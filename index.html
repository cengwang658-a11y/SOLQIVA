WHY SOLQIVA

Security should make sense
to the person signing.

A wallet prompt can ask for a signature without making its consequences clear. Our goal is to give everyday users and small teams an understandable view of asset movements, permissions, and potential risks before they approve.

A familiar safety center for your Solana wallet.

See what could leave your wallet. Understand who gets access. Decide whether to proceed. SOLQIVA brings the clarity of a consumer payment app to the design of an onchain security advisor.

⌕

See the risk before signing

Planned transaction previews would explain expected asset movements and flag suspicious recipients or permission requests before you approve.

▤

Understand the warning

Plain-language explanations instead of an unexplained score. Chinese-language guidance is a planned priority for users and builders entering the Solana ecosystem.

⌘

Keep agent access bounded

Explore spending limits, read-only access, and permission reviews. The goal is to make it clear what each wallet connection or AI agent is allowed to do.

Built around individuals and small teams

Our focus is the moment a person interacts with an application: a transfer, a token transaction, a new permission, or an agent spending request. This user-facing layer is intended to complement protocol audits, monitoring, and incident response.

IN DEVELOPMENT

A working interface. A clear next step.

Today you can explore a browser-based demo with sample balances, risk reports, agent controls, and a simulated community globe. Live wallet integration, transaction simulation, risk-data sources, and Chinese-language explanations are planned work.

Does this demo protect or hold real funds?

No. This version does not connect a live wallet, hold funds, intercept transactions, or change onchain permissions. The reserve and release controls only adjust sample balances within the demo.

Is SOLQIVA an official Solana or Alipay product?

No. SOLQIVA is an independent project. The consumer safety-center concept is a usability reference, not a partnership or endorsement by Solana Foundation or Alipay.

Can a risk check guarantee that a transaction is safe?

No. Risk signals can be incomplete, and simulation cannot rule out every threat. The planned product should explain its findings and limitations so users can make an informed decision.
